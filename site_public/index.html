How Clipoa uses your YouTube account

When you connect YouTube, Google requests only two permissions:

  • Upload videos (youtube.upload) — uploads only the short videos you select, with your title and caption, at the time you choose.
  • Read channel name (youtube.readonly) — displays the connected channel name and ID so you know where each short will publish.

Clipoa never deletes or edits existing videos, never reads comments or analytics, and never publishes without your action. Disconnect anytime in Clipoa or revoke access in Google Account permissions.
